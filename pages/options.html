Short-form & feeds

The most engineered-to-addict content on the internet, handled as a category.

Block all short-form content

Treat TikTok as short-form

Allow links friends send me

Instagram: messages only

Hide videos matching keywords

Keywords & categories

Any page whose address or title contains a blocked keyword gets stopped — across every site.

Tip: keywords match anywhere in the URL or page title, case-insensitive. Short generic words ("a", "the") will over-block.

Block adult content

Known adult sites and explicit keywords are blocked outright. These blocks can never be unlocked, not even with the challenge.
