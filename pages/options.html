Short videos

The most engineered format on the internet, handled as a single category.

Block all short videos Hard to undo

YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and Snapchat Spotlight — hidden in feeds and unreachable by URL. Never unlockable.

Treat TikTok as short videos Hard to undo

Blocks tiktok.com entirely.

Allow links people send me

A short, reel or post opened from a DM, another app or a pasted link plays — just that one, for five minutes. Messages from different people each open on their own, so a busy inbox isn't one link and done; within a single conversation it's one item every ten minutes. Swiping to the next is still blocked, and an item you've already watched doesn't open twice.

Lighter touch

Decluttering rather than blocking. These switch back at any time, with no challenge.

LinkedIn: hide Home and My Network

The two nav buttons that lead to the feed disappear. Messaging, jobs and notifications stay where they are.

Twitch: calm it down

No front-page carousel, no recommended channels or categories, no Browse, Following or For You tabs, no notification bell. Your followed channels in the sidebar and the search bar still work.

News

Read the article you came for, not the ten the page lines up next.

Calm news outlets

On major news sites, North strips the noise around the story: "recommended for you", trending and most-read rails, "more from", and the Taboola and Outbrain modules at the foot of the page. The article, search and sections stay. Switches back at any time, with no challenge.

Covers the major outlets: the New York Times, the Guardian, the BBC, CNN, the Washington Post, Reuters, the Verge and dozens more. If a site you read isn't covered, name it in the suggestion box under Preferences.

YouTube

Keep the useful parts; remove the parts designed to keep you there. Shorts are handled under Short videos.

Focus tools

Each switch removes one hook.

Hide the sidebar

The whole left rail goes: home shortcuts, Explore, the channel list. The search bar stays, and your history, liked videos and playlists still open by URL or from the profile menu.

Calm the home feed

The gentle option: keeps the first three recommendations, drops the All / Gaming / Music chip bar, and shrinks the thumbnails. Ignored if you hide the feed entirely below.

Hide the home feed

The strict option: replaces recommendations entirely with a prompt to search deliberately. Overrides "Calm the home feed" above.

Hide related videos

No "up next" sidebar, and no wall of recommendations when a video ends.

Hide comments

Watch the video, skip the thread underneath it.

Hide subscriptions

Removes the subscriptions feed. If a channel matters to you, you'll remember it.

Topic mode

Choose the topics you actually want from YouTube — guitar, coding, workouts. Feeds and search results only show videos that match. Direct links still open, so a link someone sends you won't break.

Hide videos matching keywords

Videos whose title or channel matches are removed from every feed and search result.

Filters

Block pages by what they're about, not just where they live.

Blocked keywords

Any page whose address or title contains one of these is blocked, on every site.

Keywords match anywhere in the URL or page title, case-insensitively. Very short or generic words will over-block.

18+ content Always blocked

Known adult sites and explicit keywords are blocked outright, in feeds and by URL. This protection is built into North and cannot be switched off, not even with the challenge.

Free for life

North will never charge you, show you an advert, or sell a premium version of self-control. Blockers that paywall the features that actually work are part of the problem. If North has bought you back some hours and you'd like to keep it going, tips are the entire business model.

The fine print

Everything North knows stays on your device: no accounts, no tracking, and no data leaving your browser. And yes, you can always uninstall the extension. North's job is to interrupt a habit, not to trap you — every second of friction is a chance for intention to catch up.
