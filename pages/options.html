Shorts & feeds

The most engineered-to-addict content on the internet, handled as a category.

Block all short videos

YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, Snapchat Spotlight. Hidden in feeds and unreachable by URL. Never unlockable.

Treat TikTok as short videos

Allow links friends send me

A short, reel or post opened from a DM, another app or a pasted link plays. Just that one, for 5 minutes, at most one new pass every 10 minutes. Swiping to the next is still blocked.

Instagram: messages only

DMs stay fully available. The feed, Explore, Reels and profiles are blocked, and instagram.com goes straight to your inbox.

18+ content Always blocked

Known adult sites and explicit keywords are blocked outright, in feeds and by URL. This protection is built into North and can't be switched off — not even with the challenge.

Topic mode: only what I came for

Pick the topics you actually want from YouTube, like guitar, coding or workouts. Feeds and search results only show videos that match. Direct links still open, so a friend's link won't break.

Hide videos matching keywords
